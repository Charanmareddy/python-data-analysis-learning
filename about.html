ABOUT DATALAB

Clear lessons for curious analysts.

DataLab is a practical, beginner-friendly path through the Python tools used in everyday data analysis.

Learning should feel approachable

You do not need a computer science background to start. Each guide introduces the idea in plain language, connects it to a familiar dataset, and gives you a small step to try.

Learn by doing

Examples use employee, student, sales, hospital, and customer scenarios to show why a technique matters.

One tool at a time

Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, and Seaborn have separate guides, with their own methods and examples.

Build good habits

Understand inputs, outputs, assumptions, and common mistakes—not just which code to copy.

Practice with purpose

Use short questions, coding exercises, and mini projects to turn concepts into working knowledge.

Suggested route

  1. Start with PandasGet comfortable with tables and real-world records.
  2. Meet NumPyUnderstand arrays and numerical calculations.
  3. Visualize with MatplotlibBuild charts with control over the details.
  4. Explore with SeabornUse statistical charts to investigate patterns.
